Happy Bhai Dooj

बगीचे में मस्ती

Bagiche mein bhaiya aur behna ki catch-catch, hansi aur dher saari masti.

दौड़म-भाग

Aage behna, peeche bhaiya, patang ke saath dono daud pade.

Behna ne tilak lagaya, bhai ne vaada kiya: hamesha saath rahenge, jhoole ki tarah, upar-neeche, har mod par.
